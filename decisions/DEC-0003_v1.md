# DEC-0003

## Decision

A human ingest with no stated authorization basis is PROVENANCE_UNCERTAIN until a basis is given, not USER_ESTABLISHED.

---

## Date

2026-10-09

---

## Context

CCCSystem.ingest gave any human actor the status USER_ESTABLISHED when the caller passed no provenance status and no authorization basis. USER_ESTABLISHED is the strongest human-origin status, and it makes material usable as evidence. A caller who stated nothing was still given that label.

PR #28 (commit b11871a) removed an invented basis phrase, so the record now says "not stated by caller." It deliberately left the status unchanged. That left the label itself as the unsupported claim. The decision was made by William N. King on 2026-10-09.

---

## Options Considered

A.

Keep USER_ESTABLISHED for an unstated human ingest. The record marks the basis as not stated, and nothing else changes.

B.

Use PROVENANCE_UNCERTAIN for an unstated human ingest until a basis is stated. A stated basis still gives USER_ESTABLISHED.

C.

Use UNRESOLVED for an unstated human ingest.

---

## Selected Option

B. The status is uncertain when the caller has not established it. The label, not only a metadata flag, carries that doubt, because readers see the status first.

---

## Reason Selected

The strongest human-origin label should require a stated basis, the same way a machine proposal needs an explicit human adoption before it gains user-level status (CCC-PROVENANCE-002). B makes the label match what was actually stated. It keeps a stated basis as the path to USER_ESTABLISHED, so no human who states a basis loses anything.

---

## Alternatives Rejected

A. The status would keep claiming a human established the material when nothing says so. A metadata flag is easy to miss, and the rule check only confirmed the actor was human, not that anything was established.

C. UNRESOLVED treats a human's statement with no basis the same as a question the system could not answer. That is broader than the gap. It would also change more behavior than needed for this step.

---

## Alternatives Deferred

C (UNRESOLVED) is deferred. It would return if uncertain human material needs a label distinct from uncertain machine material. Revisit it if the two cases start being confused in reviews or audits.

Whether an explicit USER_ESTABLISHED status with no basis should be refused outright, rather than marked, is deferred. Today the caller's explicit status is honored and the record is marked. Revisit it if callers start passing user-level status without a basis.

---

## Assumptions

- A basis string is a human-supplied record of why the material is user-established, and callers who need user-level status will supply one.
- No external consumer relies on the old unstated default. Evidence checks are the main consumer, and they now exclude unstated human material.
- The "not stated by caller" marker is applied to user-level statuses without a basis, and to unstated human ingests with no machine lineage. Uncertain human material with machine lineage is not marked, since its status already carries the doubt.

If one of these turns out false, revisit the decision.

---

## Expected Impact

- Unstated human ingests are PROVENANCE_UNCERTAIN, and are not usable as evidence until a basis is given.
- Stated-basis ingests, model and system ingests, and explicit caller statuses are unchanged.
- Test fixtures that relied on unstated human material as evidence now state a basis. Their assertions are unchanged.
- One new test covers an explicit user-level status over machine lineage with no basis, which is marked.

Implemented in PR #32 (branch claude/unstated-human-provenance-uncertain), merged to main on 2026-10-09.
